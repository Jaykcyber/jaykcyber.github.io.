      Understanding AI Synthetics & Deepfakes

      How modern generative models create synthetic humans, and the microscopic mathematical flaws they leave behind.

      How AI Generates Imagery

      Generative Diffusion Models (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Flux) begin with pure Gaussian noise and reverse the noise process step-by-step guided by text embeddings. Because synthesis is statistical rather than optical, fine physics and biological symmetry often break down.

      The "Digital Arrest" Scams in India

      Fraudsters use fake police backdrops, synthetic uniforms, and deepfake face-swapping over Skype or WhatsApp to stage "arrest warrants" for courier drugs or money laundering. Crucial Fact: Indian police and courts never arrest or try citizens over video calls.

      Voice Cloning & Emergency Scams

      With as little as 3 seconds of audio scraped from social media videos, AI voice cloners replicate a family member's vocal timbre to make urgent fake calls claiming accidents or arrests. Always establish a family "emergency safe-word" to verify genuine identity.

      8 Forensic Red Flags to Spot AI Images with the Naked Eye

      Even bleeding-edge generators consistently leave these 8 visual imperfections:

      1 Hands & Skeletal Digits

      Extra fingers (polydactyly), webbed knuckles, impossible joint bends, or fingernails blending into skin.

      2 Eye Specular Glints

      Real cameras reflect the same room light in both eyes. AI produces mismatched reflections and distorted pupil circles.

      3 Accessory Asymmetry

      Inspect earrings, spectacle frames, cufflinks, and collar insignias. AI frequently draws two completely different shapes.

      4 Hair & Skin Dissolution

      Flyaway hair abruptly vanishes or merges into shirt collars. Skin exhibits a waxy, porcelain sheen lacking genuine dermal pores.

      5 Background Signage

      Look at street boards, vehicle plates, or book spines in the backdrop. Diffusion renders pseudo-alien gibberish letters.

      6 Teeth & Mouth Interior

      Teeth appear fused like a solid white block without natural gum lines, or individual incisors are abnormally multiplied.

      7 Lighting Physics

      Cast shadows pointing in directions opposite to the main sun or lamp highlight, or subjects casting zero ground shadow.

      8 Periodic FFT Spikes

      In the frequency domain (2D FFT), neural upsamplers create periodic geometric star spikes invisible to human eyes.

      Indian Cyber Jurisprudence

      Legal Protections Against AI Crimes in India

      Know your statutory rights under the Information Technology Act (2000), IT Rules (2021), and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).

      Section 66E IT Act

      Violation of Bodily Privacy

      Punishes capturing, transmitting, or publishing images of private areas of any person without consent. Imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to Rs. 2 Lakhs.

      Applies to: Deepfake morphed photographs and private videos.
      Section 67 / 67A IT Act

      Sexually Explicit Content

      Publishing or transmitting sexually explicit material in electronic form. Stringent non-bailable offense: first conviction carries up to 5 years imprisonment and Rs. 10 Lakhs fine.

      Applies to: Non-consensual synthetic pornography & sextortion.
      IT Rules 2021 — Rule 3(2)(b)

      24-Hour Mandatory Platform Takedown

      Social media platforms (Intermediaries) MUST take down or disable access to non-consensual morphed sexual imagery or impersonation within 24 hours of receiving user notice.

Understanding AI Synthetics & Deepfakes

Generative AI technologies have advanced to photorealistic fidelity. Learn the core mechanics, visual flaws, and scam vectors to protect yourself and your community.

How Generative AI Works

Modern AI generators (Diffusion models like Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, and GANs) do not "copy and paste" images. They learn statistical patterns of noise and reconstruct images from text prompts. Because they synthesize textures probabilistically, they often leave microscopic edge and frequency flaws.

Prevalent Cyber Threats

Malicious actors leverage deepfakes for "Digital Arrest" extortion (impersonating police officers), non-consensual synthetic imagery for blackmail, AI voice cloning for urgent financial transfers, and fake matrimonial profiles created with stolen likenesses.

The Zero-Trust Mindset

Never verify an emergency request for funds over video or voice alone. Always cross-verify through a secondary channel or trusted mutual contact. Never share OTPs or download screen-sharing apps when receiving unexpected urgent calls.

6 Forensic Red Flags: How to Visually Spot a Fake

Even high-end AI generators leave tell-tale visual inconsistencies:

Play Real vs AI Quiz
1 Hands, Fingers & Teeth

AI struggles with complex skeletal biomechanics. Look for extra digits, webbed knuckles, unnatural joint angles, or teeth that lack individual separation.

2 Eye Specular Reflections

In authentic photography, light reflections in both eyes match the room's light sources. AI often generates mismatched glints, distorted pupils, or asymmetric irises.

3 Earrings & Symmetrical Wear

Check paired accessories like spectacles, earrings, collar pins, and cufflinks. AI portraits frequently generate completely different earring shapes on each ear.

4 Hair Strands & Skin Texture

AI skin often appears unnaturally plastic, airbrushed, or waxy. Individual hair strands may abruptly dissolve into the background or merge into clothing textures.

5 Background Text & Signage

Look at street signs, book spines, or shirt logos in the background. Diffusion models notoriously render gibberish alien glyphs instead of coherent typography.

6 Lighting Physics & Shadows

Notice if cast shadows match the apparent primary light source. AI models often generate conflicting highlights on the nose and cheeks from nonexistent angles.

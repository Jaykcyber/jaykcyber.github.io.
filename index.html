REALTRACE AI

A Generalizable Forensic Intelligence Framework for Visual Authenticity Verification & Digital Citizen Defense

Protecting Digital India from Synthetic Media & Fraud

RealTrace AI is a multi-modal forensic framework developed at Engineering College Ajmer Rajasthan under the Information Security Education and Awareness (ISEA) project, supported by C-DAC and the Ministry of Electronics & IT, Government of India.

By integrating state-of-the-art deep learning architectures (Baseline CNN, ResNet-18, and Robust EfficientNet-B0) with spatial Grad-CAM explainability, 2D FFT/DCT frequency-domain analysis, and real-time SMS/URL phishing scanners, RealTrace AI empowers citizens and forensic analysts to verify visual content with scientific precision.
